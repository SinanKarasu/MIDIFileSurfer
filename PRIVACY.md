# MIDIFileSurfer Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

MIDIFileSurfer is a local MIDI file browser, player, and inspector developed by Sinan Karasu. It does not collect or transmit your MIDI files, personal information, or usage analytics to the developer. It contains no advertising, tracking, or account system.

## Files and local settings

Files you select are read and processed on your Mac. The app stores preferences, recently used folder paths, and macOS file-access bookmarks locally to remember folders you have authorized. The Finder Quick Look extension reads the file being previewed locally. MIDIFileSurfer does not modify your original MIDI files.

You can remove the app's saved settings and file-access bookmarks by deleting its application container after quitting the app. This does not delete your original MIDI files.

## Feedback and external services

If you choose to contact the developer or submit a GitHub issue, the information you send is used to respond and investigate the issue. GitHub issues are public: do not include private information or files you cannot share. Email, GitHub, Apple's App Store, and TestFlight are governed by their respective providers' privacy policies. Apple may provide diagnostics and beta feedback according to your Apple settings and TestFlight choices.
